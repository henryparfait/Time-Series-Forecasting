# Data licence and attribution

The files in this folder are **derived** from the Telecom Italia "Telecommunications - SMS, Call, Internet - MI"
dataset (Harvard Dataverse, doi:10.7910/DVN/EGZHFV) and the Milano grid (doi:10.7910/DVN/QJWLFU).

The source data is released under the **Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0**
(<http://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/>). As required by the licence, these derived files are shared
under the same ODbL 1.0 terms.

Data [from BigDataChallenge contest](http://www.telecomitalia.com/tit/en/bigdatachallenge.html),
[ODI node Trento](http://theodi.fbk.eu/).

Reference: G. Barlacchi *et al.*, "A multi-source dataset of urban life in the city of Milan and the Province of
Trentino," *Sci. Data*, vol. 2, Art. no. 150055, 2015, doi:10.1038/sdata.2015.55.
